The XOR Problem & Linear Non-Separability

In 1969, Minsky & Papert proved that a single-layer perceptron cannot learn an XOR function, triggering the first "AI Winter".

2D Binary Input Space Plot

Dots represent inputs: ● Output 1 | ● Output 0

Why Single Neurons Fail at XOR

Mathematical proof of linear decision boundaries.

A single artificial neuron computes a linear decision boundary equation:

w₁ · x₁ + w₂ · x₂ + b = 0

For AND or OR gates, a single straight line easily divides the 1s from the 0s.

For XOR, the output 1s are at (0,1) and (1,0), while 0s are at (0,0) and (1,1). It is geometrically impossible for any single straight line to separate these diagonal points!

The Solution: Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs)

By adding a hidden layer with non-linear activation functions (or combining NAND + OR through an AND gate), neural networks construct curved or multiple decision lines to solve XOR!

Minsky & Papert (1969) "Perceptrons"

Perceptron Learning Rule (Rosenblatt, 1957)

wᵢ ← wᵢ + α · (target - predicted) · xᵢ

When target matches prediction, error is 0 and weights remain unchanged. If predictions differ, weights shift proportionally to reduce error.

Historical AI & Compute Evolution

Trace the historic leap from 19th-century logic gates to modern Transformer Large Language Models.

1854 George Boole

Boolean Algebra

Published "The Laws of Thought", formalizing binary logic (1s and 0s) as mathematical operations (AND, OR, NOT).

1943 McCulloch & Pitts

First Artificial Neuron Model

Showed that simplified mathematical neuron models could calculate any logical function (AND, OR, NOT), linking biological brain theory to computer science.

1957 Frank Rosenblatt

The Rosenblatt Perceptron

Built the Mark I Perceptron machine—the first neural model that learned custom weights from raw data via iterative feedback!

1969 Minsky & Papert

The XOR Crisis & First AI Winter

Proved single-layer perceptrons could not compute non-linearly separable functions like XOR, causing neural network funding to collapse for over a decade.

1986 Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams

Backpropagation Breakthrough

Popularized the backpropagation algorithm, enabling Multi-Layer Neural Networks (MLPs) to learn complex non-linear representations and overcome the XOR barrier permanently.

2017 - Present Modern Deep Learning

Transformers & Billions of Perceptron Parameters

Today's Large Language Models (LLMs) connect hundreds of billions of digital neurons (MLPs + Attention layers), built upon the foundational boolean and linear principles you just tested!
